Professional Profile

A SysAdmin and Security Engineer with 4+ years of experience in enterprise environments. Specializes in building, maintaining, and hardening Windows Server systems from 2008 through 2022, including Active Directory, GPO, DNS, DHCP, and secure baselines. Manages enterprise print servers and department-based printer deployment through GPO.

I take a proactive approach focused on real metrics: service availability, response time, and continuous documentation. In recent years, I upgraded network infrastructure from approximately 32 Mbps connectivity to 300/300 Mbps, alongside access-point upgrades and wireless coverage validation; and strengthened server stability through maintenance, monitoring, and infrastructure hardening, while implementing control processes that support faster incident handling.

I also specialize in building computers to end-user requirements, including requirements analysis, component selection, budget/performance balancing, stress testing, and thermal tuning. I have built more than 350 computers in my work, with documentation and complete load testing.
