About Me in Brief

A SysAdmin and Security Engineer with more than five years of experience in enterprise environments. Specializes in building, maintaining, and hardening Windows Server systems from 2008 through 2022, including Active Directory, GPO, DNS, DHCP, and secure baselines. Manages enterprise print servers and department-based printer deployment through GPO.

I take a proactive approach focused on real metrics: service availability, response time, and continuous documentation. In recent years, I improved network availability by approximately 20%, reduced server crashes by more than 40%, and implemented monitoring processes that support faster incident handling.

I also specialize in building computers to end-user requirements, including requirements analysis, component selection, budget/performance balancing, stress testing, and thermal tuning. I have built more than 350 computers in my work, with documentation and complete load testing.
