Blue Team: Defense Exercises

Two short exercises in defensive thinking: spot sign-ins that deserve a closer look, and pick the right step at each stage of an incident.

All data on this page is entirely fictional and for learning only. There are no real systems here, and nothing is stored or sent.

Exercise 1: Sign-in triage

Four fictional sign-in records. For each one, decide whether it needs review or shows no immediate concern. Your first choice locks. Each correct answer earns 250 points.
