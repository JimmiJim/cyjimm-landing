FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who do I actually work with?

Directly with Matan Jamil, who scopes and delivers the work. There are no departments or teams behind CyJimm.

How is pricing set?

By the agreed scope of work, after an initial needs review.

How long does a security project take?

It depends on the environment and the scope. With a clearly defined scope, a Security Baseline can sometimes be done in a few weeks.

What is needed before a penetration test?

A scope and authorization agreed in advance. Tests run only within what was agreed.

What should the first message include?

A short description of the environment and the need is enough to start. Do not include passwords or sensitive information.

Is there a service for home users or small businesses?

Yes, through Jimmi's Hub: computer diagnostics and repair, Windows, upgrades, backup, and remote support.
