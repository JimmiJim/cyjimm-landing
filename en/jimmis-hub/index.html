Jimmi's Hub is CyJimm's service area for individuals, home users, freelancers, and small businesses that need focused help with computers, Windows, upgrades, backup, networking, basic security, and remote support.

How We Can Help

Every service starts with understanding the fault and how the computer is used, followed by a practical solution-without replacing hardware unless it is genuinely necessary.

Repair & Diagnostics

  • Laptop and desktop diagnostics and repair.
  • Slow, frozen, unstable, or crashing computers.
  • BSOD and system-crash analysis.
  • Disk-health and SMART checks.
  • Laptop battery-health checks.
  • Overheating, fan, and thermal-performance checks.
  • Basic physical cleaning and thermal-paste replacement when relevant.

Windows & Software

  • Windows troubleshooting.
  • Windows 10/11 reinstallations and clean installations.
  • Operating-system upgrades and startup-performance improvements.
  • Driver installation and vendor updates.
  • BIOS and Firmware updates when required.
  • Software installation and workspace configuration.
  • Removal of unwanted software and bloatware.
  • Office, Microsoft 365, Outlook, and email troubleshooting.

Hardware Upgrades

  • RAM upgrades.
  • SSD/NVMe installation or replacement.
  • Storage upgrades and expansion.
  • Hardware compatibility checks before purchase.
  • Advice before buying a computer or upgrading components.

Security & Malware Removal

  • Removal of viruses, Malware, Adware, and Potentially Unwanted Programs.
  • Basic personal-computer security checks.
  • Removal of unwanted components and basic security hardening.
  • Official vendor tools and updates whenever practical.

Backup, Recovery & Data Migration

  • Data migration from an old computer to a new one.
  • File backup and recovery when the storage condition allows it.
  • Workspace restoration after replacing a computer.
  • Migration of users, files, browsers, and supported passwords between computers.
  • Backup before significant operations whenever practical.

New PC Setup

  • New-computer setup from the box to a complete working environment.
  • Installation of common software based on the user's needs.
  • Account, update, security, and workspace configuration.
  • Restoration of files and the working environment from the previous computer.

Network, Printers & Peripherals

  • Home Wi-Fi and network troubleshooting.
  • Printer, scanner, and peripheral setup.
  • Bluetooth, camera, microphone, and audio troubleshooting.
  • OneDrive, Dropbox, and Cloud Storage configuration.

Remote Support

  • Remote assistance when an on-site visit is not required.
  • Initial diagnosis, software troubleshooting, and configuration.
  • Clear explanations during and after the work.

How It Works

A clear, structured process so you understand what was found and what is proposed before significant work begins.

1

Get in touch and describe the problem.

2

Initial diagnosis.

3

Clear explanation of the findings and required work.

4

Your approval before significant work.

5

Repair, upgrade, or installation.

6

Complete testing before handover.

7

A short explanation of what was done.

Professional, Transparent Service

The goal is to understand the source of the problem, explain the options clearly, and perform only the work that is needed.

  • No hardware replacement without a genuine need.
  • Customer information and privacy are protected throughout the work.
  • Backups are made before significant operations whenever practical.
  • Clear explanations for non-technical users without hiding important details.
  • Root-cause analysis instead of treating only the symptom.
  • Official vendor tools, drivers, and updates whenever practical.
