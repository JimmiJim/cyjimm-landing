Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-22

We respect users' privacy and do not collect more information than necessary. This website does not use tracking cookies and does not load third-party scripts for advertising or advertising profiling.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details submitted through the contact form (name, email, company, and message content).
  • Basic traffic and performance data associated with serving the website, via Cloudflare Web Analytics (see below).

How We Use Information

  • Responding to inquiries and providing services.
  • Improving the website and our services.
  • Understanding site traffic and delivery performance.

Processing and Subprocessors

The form is managed by FormSubmit as a data processor. Information may be transferred to FormSubmit servers to deliver the email message. We recommend reviewing its privacy policy.

The website is served through Cloudflare. Cloudflare Web Analytics provides traffic and performance metrics associated with serving the site. It is infrastructure-provider analytics, not advertising or advertising profiling. This website does not use Google Analytics.

Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was provided or as required by law.

Data Subject Rights

Under applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. For inquiries: privacy@cyjimm.com.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS. We apply reasonable controls to protect data and limit exposure to necessary third-party services only.

Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@cyjimm.com
